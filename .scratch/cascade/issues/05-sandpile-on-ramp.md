## The question

Fire's on-ramp works by **planting a linear expectation and shattering it** — beat 2 is the trap (ticket 02). The sandpile has no user-owned dial to trap them with, and its aha is *"nobody set the dial."* So: **what is the sandpile's trap?**

Open threads this must settle:

- Where does the **~12 s charge** (ticket 04, fork 4) live — is it beat 1, or a pre-beat that runs while the caption sets up the expectation?
- The fire on-ramp made the user **act every beat**. What is the sandpile's act during a charge they are watching?
- Is the trap **"feed it harder and watch the dial refuse"** (the futility experiment, rejected as a *control* in ticket 04 but still available as a *beat*), or **"predict the next avalanche and be wrong"**?
- When does the **plot's straight line** resolve — the fire's knee resolved *in sync* with the crossing (ticket 02); what is the sandpile's synchronised moment?
- Release gate to the sandbox, skip affordance, `localStorage` re-entry (fire pattern: `cascade.ff.onramp`).
